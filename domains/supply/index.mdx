## Overview

The supply domain knows the shelves. In the demo story, the warehouse reserves the stock for
order 4711 and hands it over to the third-party logistics flow.

## Business keys

`orderid`, `customerid`

## Services and events

Services and events of this domain appear here automatically as soon as their repository
releases a contract.

## Data model

What the WMS knows: stock per product and warehouse, and the reservation for each order. Kept in the simulated WMS; the WMS System API translates.
