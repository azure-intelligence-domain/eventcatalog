## Overview

The [customer portal](https://azure-intelligence-domain.github.io/customer-portal/) (Vue.js, [repository](https://github.com/azure-intelligence-domain/customer-portal)) calls only the Experience APIs, through the gateway. The Experience APIs decide nothing: they shape what the order domain and the System APIs say, for the people who use it.
