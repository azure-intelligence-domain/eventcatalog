## Overview

The channel domain shapes the platform for the people who use it (ADR 0017). Its Experience APIs
**decide nothing**: they read what the Process and System APIs know, through the gateway, and pass
commands to the order domain, which decides.

| Experience API | For | Built as |
|---|---|---|
| Webshop | Shopping for a customer, following an order end to end, paying its invoice | A Function composing Order, Salesforce, SAP and the 3PL |
| Customer Service | Any customer at a glance, any order with what every domain knows; hold, release, reschedule, cancel. Also an AI agent's tool (agent gateway) | A Function composing Order, Salesforce, SAP, WMS and the 3PL |
| Partner | The 3PL partner's bookings, pick-up and delivery notices | No code: the gateway serves part of the 3PL System API's contract |

The [customer portal](https://azure-intelligence-domain.github.io/customer-portal/) (Vue.js) is
their front end: staff sign in with Google (`thevaluehub.be` accounts only), and every click is a
business journey (`Correlation-Id: ord-<orderId>`) in the tap.

## Business keys

`orderid`, `customerid`: the channel adds none of its own.

## Services and events

Services of this domain appear here automatically as soon as their repository releases a contract.
The channel publishes no events: what its users do becomes the order domain's facts.

## Data model

None of its own: the channel shows the order domain's orders with the finance, supply and
logistics domains' documents around them.
