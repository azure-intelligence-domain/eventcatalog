Salesforce's Order record: the system of record of every order. Salesforce naming: PascalCase and __c custom fields.

Order, OrderItem, Account and Product are simulated in Cosmos DB (backend-systems, system-api-salesforce 1.1.0); Opportunity is not simulated yet.
