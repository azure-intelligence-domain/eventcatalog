## Overview

The finance domain knows what is invoiced and paid. In the demo story, SAP confirms order 4711
and has the invoice ready.

## Business keys

`customerid`, `orderid`

## Services and events

Services and events of this domain appear here automatically as soon as their repository
releases a contract.

## Data model

What SAP knows about an order once it reached the ERP: the sales order, its invoice and the payments against it. Kept in the simulated SAP, in SAP's own tables; the SAP System API translates.
