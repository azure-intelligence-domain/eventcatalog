## Overview

The order domain team owns the Order Process API and the order's business events (OrderPlaced,
OrderConfirmed, OrderCancelled, OrderPaid, ...). It turns facts from finance, supply and logistics
into order facts through policies, and publishes every change with orderid and customerid.

Repositories in the [azure-intelligence-domain](https://github.com/azure-intelligence-domain) organisation.
