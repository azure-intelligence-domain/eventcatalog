## Overview

The channel team shapes the platform for the people who use it: customers through the webshop,
customer service, and the 3PL partner. It owns the Experience APIs and the customer portal, and
decides nothing about an order: the order domain does.

Repositories in the [azure-intelligence-domain](https://github.com/azure-intelligence-domain) organisation.
